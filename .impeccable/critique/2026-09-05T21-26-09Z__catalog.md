## Priority Issues

**[P1] Prism · Hold is two plugins and two clocks**
- **Why:** Inspect-then-compose becomes a wall; Time dial and Division pads look like two params.
- **Fix:** One plate per story, or dim the unused encoding so one control is the source.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Insert In clips to I; Match is a blank moss square**
- **Why:** First scan of the composed faceplate fails recognition.
- **Fix:** Readable meter caption outside the 1.35rem column; mark on Match or a labeled pad.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Rest / Home / End / Shift-fine are invisible**
- **Why:** Power users find them; a designer shipping the kit will not know they are the language.
- **Fix:** One line on Knob stories or a rest tick on the ring — not a tutorial modal.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] Story name and Ladle theme still lie**
- **Why:** Nav shows Prism hold; lightbulb can recast fog despite theme addon disabled in config.
- **Fix:** Force the middot in the tree; neutralize Ladle color-scheme so it cannot retheme `--page`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P3] Compact hits are postage stamps in a 400px field; twin Mix at 35%**
- **Why:** Size inspection is harder; Hold Mix reads as copy-paste.
- **Fix:** Scale the frame to the size being demoed; differentiate Hold Mix.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`
