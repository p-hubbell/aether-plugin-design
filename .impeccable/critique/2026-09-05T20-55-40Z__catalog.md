## Design Specificity Verdict

**Start here.** Authored for Aether, not a generic plugin skin — then the dual brief (wispy field + pocket instrument) fails in use.

**LLM assessment:** Fog, charcoal ink, moss-as-latch, 1px squares, rectangular rotors, Nunito chrome, VT323 in the field, Italianno as one watermark word. Ladle stays inspector chrome. That is a real family. Isolated Knob · Dial still floats a schematic needle on mist with no Van de Graaf plate. The veil sits at sparse mute density; the repeating wisp reads as CAD graph paper. Hits feel like diagram ticks, not objects under a lamp. The language is recognizable; the memorable moment (hit on a plate with a slow ASCII veil) barely happens.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json catalog` and `detect.mjs --json src` both returned `[]` (exit 0), including `--no-config`. 16 catalog and 24 src markup files scanned. Zero rule hits; no false positives to discount. The detector did not see faint atmosphere, 100px drag mapping, 40px knobs, or the inert Hold Time control — those are operate and visual issues outside static CSS/class heuristics.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay. Browser mutation preflight failed (cursor-ide-browser could not keep a navigable tab). live-server was not started; `detect.js` was not injected. Fallback: CLI scan plus headless Chrome / CDP on Ladle at `http://127.0.0.1:61005/`.

## Overall Impression

The kit kept its paint laws and finally put ASCII back behind ThemeRoot. Operate still feels like a schematic inspector: Dial is a gray square on gray mist, one short drag can peg Rate, and the north-star veil is a rumor behind the plate. Biggest opportunity: make the memorable moment the default scene — a hittable instrument on a plate with a veil you can actually see — without abandoning 1px squares or the fog/ink/moss palette.

## What's Working

- Paint and shape rules hold: fog `#eef1f4`, ink, mute, moss `#4f6458`, radius 0, no TE orange, no drop shadow, no glass.
- Live knobs tell the truth: Rate 440 Hz went to 2000 Hz with the rotor at travel end.
- Faceplate · Insert is the only beat that starts to feel like a family plate (hero Drive, rails, Match moss invert, Tone Lo/Hi).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Atmosphere vs hit**
- **Why it matters:** Seto cannot judge whether the memorable moment survives on a plugin faceplate; ethereal refs and pocket-instrument refs cancel.
- **Fix:** Raise ASCII contrast and keep slow motion so glyphs read through plate haze; retune or drop the repeating-linear wisp so it is mist, not graph paper; thicken the physical read of the hit without rounding it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable bolder`

**[P1] Vertical drag is hypersensitive**
- **Why it matters:** `PIXELS_FOR_FULL_RANGE = 100` turned a ~90px drag into 440→2000 Hz. No undo/default. Feels like a timeline fader pretending to be a knob.
- **Fix:** Longer throw, grabbed state, restore-to-initial, readable travel.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Analog hits miss the 44px floor**
- **Why it matters:** Pads/steppers keep 2.75rem; default knob is 2.5rem (40px), slider hit 1.5rem (24px). Insert Tone and Prism EQ/Feedback ship the small knob. AA is promised then skipped on the controls that will sit on a faceplate.
- **Fix:** Floor analog hits at 2.75rem, or mark mini as display-only and stop using it on Insert.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Hold Time looks live and is dead**
- **Why it matters:** Same Dial silhouette as Cutoff, `onChange` missing when synced; dragging does nothing.
- **Fix:** Drive Time from Division, or freeze it as a readout (no pointer, caption that it follows Division).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Knob story and hover language are incomplete**
- **Why it matters:** Size scale used on Insert never appears on Knob; pad invert vs knob moss outline teaches two products; Ladle theme can leave the fog world.
- **Fix:** Export knob/dial/hero in the Knob story; unify hover to the invert rule; keep Ladle theme off this catalog.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** Open Dial, slam Rate, jump to Insert/Hold. 100px full-range drag; no default/undo; no wheel; Hold Time looks draggable and is inert; Shift-fine is undocumented; nine-story sidebar with no documented keyboard story jump.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** Tab to Rate, arrows, then Insert Match / Mix. Tone/EQ/Feedback at 40px; Mix slider 24px tall; meters expose 0–1 without a live region when Match changes Out; choice pads park tab on the checked radio only.

**Seto (plugin designer):** Operate one kit control, then open Faceplate or Prism · Hold. Dial never sits on a Van de Graaf plate, so beat two is a context switch. Overlay defaults off. Insert uses three knob sizes the Knob story never showed. Squares still read as a wireframe kit, not a lamp-lit instrument.
