## Design Specificity Verdict

**Start here.** Authored for Aether on composed plates; isolated kit stories still wear the paint but sit like misty component demos.

**LLM assessment:** Insert, Prism, and Hold are a pocket-instrument family: 1px ink boxes, square moss rotors, latch invert, Nunito chrome, Italianno flourish, ASCII veil inside the SizeFrame, fog Ladle page. Isolated stories (Dial, Meter In, Pad Hits, Slider Mix, Stepper Mode, Nameplate Blank, Surface Construction) dump Nameplate + hit into the default Van de Graaf grid with no named regions, so the hit parks in a pad/gutter cell and the veil/flourish becomes the composition.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json catalog` and `detect.mjs --json src` both returned `[]`, exit 0. Zero rule hits, no file locations, no detector-reported false positives. The detector did not see isolated-story grid parking, Hold Time-as-liar-knob, overlay buried in Ladle args, or faint construction strokes.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay. Cursor IDE browser MCP did not register; title/script mutation and `detect.js` injection were skipped. Headless Chrome confirmed `knob--dial`, `faceplate--insert`, and `plates--hold` load (HTTP 200). Those screenshots are load signal, not overlays.

## Overall Impression

The family language is real on Faceplate and Plates. The catalog as a walk (operate one kit control, then Insert / Prism / Hold) still teaches the wrong composition on isolated stories and still lies about synced Time. Biggest opportunity: put every isolated hit on one named region, and make Hold’s clock a single encoding that looks like what it is.

## What's Working

1. Composed plates are a line, not a widget pack — Insert / Prism / Hold share nameplate, square rotor, moss-as-function, translucent hits over veil.
2. Veil clipped to SizeFrame; Ladle page is fog. Outer ThemeRoot `veil={false}` plus inner flush ThemeRoot matches the brief.
3. Analog operate affordances are real (drag, Shift, Home/End, double-click rest, 44px pads/steppers, 3px focus-visible) — they are just poorly taught outside Knob stories.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Isolated stories are not on the construction**
- **Why it matters:** Seto will copy Nameplate + hit as raw Surface children and miss Cell/areas. Dial/Meter/Pad/Slider/Construction look like empty mist stages.
- **Fix:** One named region per isolated story (name + hit in the content span). Overlay off on those stories per DESIGN.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Hold synced Time still looks grabable**
- **Why it matters:** Clock = sync: Time has no onChange, muted rotor, still a square hit. Division pads are the clock. Alex will drag Time.
- **Fix:** When synced, drop the rotor and show only the 1/8 readout (or a caption that Division sets Time). Live ms knob only for free.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Construction overlay is off-stage**
- **Why it matters:** Surface Construction, Insert, Prism, Hold default overlay false. The control is Ladle’s addon, not an on-plate chip. Lines are faint; no legend.
- **Fix:** Default Construction overlay on. 44px overlay chip on Surface/Faceplate/Prism/Hold only. Darken power lines; add DESIGN’s overlay legend.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] Catalog demos lie about pad semantics**
- **Why it matters:** Pad Hits Off/On and bank A/B share two booleans. Hold’s Division bank is exclusive and disables in free.
- **Fix:** Four independent pads, or one exclusive bank — not a coupled fake.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Operate chrome is Knob-only**
- **Why it matters:** Same gestures on Slider Mix with no caption. Insert skips CatalogStage.
- **Fix:** Same hint under every analog story; wrap Insert in CatalogStage like Prism/Hold.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** Drags Hold Time at 1/8 and gets a dead hit. Looks for overlay on the plate; finds it in Ladle controls. Keyboard exists but is advertised only on Compact/Dial/Hero. Pair is two plugins to parse, not a faster inspect.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** Analog hits expose slider roles and labelled-by chrome; veil is aria-hidden; reduced motion pauses the field. Choice pads park tab on the checked radio only. Mute on fog at 0.6875rem uppercase is the contrast risk. Hover invert is pointer-fine only. Stepper chevrons can read as black squares, not arrows.

**Seto (plugin designer):** Isolated stories do not preview region placement. Nameplate Blank / kit is not a plugin nameplate. Fixture compact/standard/wide is a buried Ladle arg. Insert is the best WebView proof; Meter/Pad/Construction teach composition badly.
